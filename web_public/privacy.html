Privacy Policy

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Abianzo. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Our core promise: We never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Abianzo ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application. By accessing or using Abianzo, you signify that you have read, understood, and agree to our collection, storage, use, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the App, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise contact us. The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the App.

  • Personal Identity Data: Name, email address, phone number.
  • Profile Data: Preferences, profile pictures, and history.
  • Device Data: IP address, device type, operating system, and App version.

3. Phone Number Authentication

To ensure a secure and verified user environment, Abianzo uses phone number authentication. When you sign up or log in, we collect your phone number and send a One-Time Password (OTP) to verify your identity.

Your phone number is strictly used for account security, account recovery, and essential service notifications. It will not be used for unsolicited marketing or shared with external marketing agencies.

4. Location Data Usage

Abianzo requires access to your location data to provide core application features such as matching you with nearby services, calculating delivery routes, and tracking ongoing orders.

  • Foreground Location: We access your location while the app is open to show real-time maps and services.
  • Background Location (if applicable): If you are a service provider (like a Rider), we may collect your location in the background to ensure accurate routing and order assignments.

You can enable or disable location services at any time through your device settings.

5. Camera Access Usage

We may request access to your device's camera and photo library. This is strictly for functionalities such as:

  • Uploading a profile picture.
  • Scanning documents (e.g., ID verification for vendor/rider onboarding).
  • Capturing images for customer support inquiries.

Images captured are transmitted securely and stored only as long as necessary to fulfill the requested feature. We do not access your camera without your explicit permission.

6. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect or receive for various business purposes, including:

  • To facilitate account creation and logon process.
  • To provide, operate, and maintain our App.
  • To improve, personalize, and expand our App features.
  • To send you administrative information, such as updates, security alerts, and support messages.
  • To protect our Services from fraudulent activity.

7. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. We use state-of-the-art encryption technologies when transferring and receiving consumer data exchanged with our site. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security over the internet.

8. Third-Party Services

Abianzo integrates with selected third-party service providers to facilitate our services. These third parties have access to your Personal Information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

  • Firebase: Used for secure user authentication (including OTP verification) and backend database management.
  • Google Maps: Used for location mapping, address autocomplete, and route optimization.

9. User Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, such as the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data.
  • Request correction of your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Withdraw consent at any time where we are relying on consent to process your data.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
