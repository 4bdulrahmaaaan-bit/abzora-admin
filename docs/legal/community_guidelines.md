# Abzora Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026

## 1. Respectful Conduct
1. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or threats.
2. Maintain professional, respectful communication across all app roles.

## 2. Anti-Fraud
1. No fake orders, fake deliveries, payment abuse, refund fraud, or identity misuse.

## 3. Illegal and Prohibited Activity
1. No illegal products/services.
2. No counterfeit or unsafe goods.
3. No unlawful content uploads.

## 4. Anti-Spam
1. No unsolicited spam, repeated promotional abuse, or misleading messages.

## 5. Safety
1. Do not share sensitive personal/payment data in public chats.
2. Report suspicious activity via support immediately.

## 6. Enforcement
1. Abzora may warn, restrict, suspend, or permanently ban accounts for violations.
