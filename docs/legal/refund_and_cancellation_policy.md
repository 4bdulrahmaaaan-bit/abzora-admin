# Abzora Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026

## 1. Eligibility for Refund
1. Order not delivered.
2. Wrong item/service delivered.
3. Damaged/defective item verified.
4. Duplicate/failed payment debit.
5. Vendor-initiated cancellation after confirmation.

## 2. Non-Refundable Cases
1. Customer misuse, false claims, or policy abuse.
2. Delivered order accepted without issue unless hidden defect is verified.
3. Delays due to force majeure events.

## 3. Cancellation Window
1. Customer may cancel before vendor acceptance/preparation, subject to item category.
2. After preparation/dispatch, cancellation may be restricted.

## 4. Refund Timeline
1. UPI/Card/Wallet refunds usually within 5-10 business days.
2. Bank delays may extend timelines; Abzora is not responsible for banking network delays.

## 5. COD Refunds
1. COD refunds are processed to bank/UPI after verification.

## 6. Partial Refunds
1. Partial refunds may be issued for missing items, quality adjustments, or service recovery.

## 7. Failed Payment Handling
1. Failed or pending transactions are auto-reconciled and reversed where applicable.

## 8. Fraud Controls
1. Repeated suspicious refund claims may trigger review, hold, or account action.

## 9. Final Decision
1. Refund decisions are made by Abzora based on evidence, logs, and vendor/rider response.
