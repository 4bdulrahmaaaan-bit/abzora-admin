# Abzora Customer Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026  
**Privacy Contact:** privacy@abzora.in  
**Support:** support@abzora.in

## 1. Definitions
1. "Personal Data" means data about an identifiable individual.
2. "Processing" means collection, use, storage, sharing, or deletion.
3. "Services" means Abzora apps and related systems.

## 2. Scope
1. This policy applies to customer app users in India.
2. It covers app, notifications, support, payments, and delivery tracking interactions.

## 3. Data We Collect
1. Identity and contact: name, mobile number, email.
2. Location data: GPS, address, delivery coordinates.
3. Device and usage data: device model, OS, app logs, diagnostics, crash data.
4. Transaction data: order history, payment method type, payment status, invoices.
5. Communication data: support chat and customer service interactions.

## 4. Technology and Service Providers
1. Firebase Authentication for login and account security.
2. Firebase Cloud Messaging for transactional notifications.
3. Firestore/Realtime Database for operational data storage.
4. Google Maps API for maps, geocoding, and route visualization.
5. Razorpay and payment partners for payment processing.
6. Analytics and error monitoring tools for reliability and performance.

## 5. Purpose of Processing
1. Account creation, OTP login, and identity verification.
2. Order processing, vendor coordination, rider assignment, and delivery fulfillment.
3. Fraud prevention, risk scoring, chargeback and abuse mitigation.
4. Notifications on order status, support, offers, and service messages.
5. Compliance, accounting, taxation (including GST), and legal obligations.

## 6. Legal Basis and Consent (DPDP)
1. We process data for specified lawful purposes with consent and legitimate business necessity.
2. Where required, we obtain your explicit consent for location and notifications.
3. You may withdraw certain permissions in app/device settings, subject to feature impact.

## 7. Data Sharing
1. With Vendors and Riders strictly as needed to fulfill orders.
2. With payment processors for transaction execution and verification.
3. With cloud and infrastructure providers under contractual safeguards.
4. With law enforcement or regulators when legally required.
5. We do not sell personal data.

## 8. Data Retention
1. Active account data is retained while account remains active.
2. Financial, tax, fraud, and legal records may be retained for statutory periods.
3. Inactive or deleted account data is removed or anonymized except required records.

## 9. Security Practices
1. Access controls, encrypted transport, role-based permissions, and audit logs.
2. Tokenized/secure payment workflows; Abzora does not store full card details.
3. Monitoring against unauthorized access and abuse attempts.

## 10. Your Rights
1. Access and review personal data.
2. Request correction of inaccurate data.
3. Request account deletion subject to legal retention requirements.
4. Request grievance review through privacy@abzora.in.

## 11. Children
1. Platform is not intended for children below legal age for independent commerce use.

## 12. International Transfers
1. If any processing occurs outside India through service providers, safeguards are applied as required by law.

## 13. IT Act and DPDP Compliance
1. Abzora aligns with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and applicable rules.
2. Abzora follows Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 obligations for consent, purpose limitation, user rights, and grievance handling.

## 14. Policy Updates
1. We may revise this policy and notify material changes through app or website.

## 15. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
1. Governed by laws of India.
2. Disputes: first grievance redressal, then arbitration in Bengaluru under Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
