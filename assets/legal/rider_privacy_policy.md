# Abianzo Rider Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026  
**Privacy Contact:** privacy@abianzo.in

## 1. Data Collected
1. Identity and KYC data.
2. Driving/vehicle/insurance data.
3. Live and historical GPS/task data.
4. Payout and bank details.
5. Device, analytics, crash diagnostics.

## 2. Purpose
1. Rider verification, dispatch allocation, safety controls, payout processing.
2. ETA calculation, fraud prevention, and quality monitoring.

## 3. Sharing
1. Limited sharing with customers/vendors for delivery execution.
2. Sharing with payment/banking providers for settlements.
3. Regulatory disclosure where required.

## 4. Retention and Rights
1. Operational and legal retention for safety, fraud, and financial compliance.
2. Access/correction/deletion requests subject to lawful exceptions.

## 5. Compliance and Security
1. IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023 aligned practices.
2. Security controls include access restrictions, encrypted transfer, and monitoring.

## 6. Contact and Dispute
1. privacy@abianzo.in for privacy requests.
2. Indian law and arbitration in Bengaluru.
