# Abianzo Vendor Terms & Conditions

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026  
**Support:** support@abianzo.in

## 1. Definitions
1. "Vendor" means an independent business onboarding on Abianzo.
2. "Commission" means platform fee charged by Abianzo.
3. "Payout" means net settlement payable after deductions.

## 2. Independent Business Relationship
1. Vendor is an independent business and not an employee/agent of Abianzo.
2. Vendor is solely responsible for business licensing, GST, statutory registrations, and legal compliance.

## 3. Onboarding and KYC
1. Vendor must provide accurate onboarding, KYC, GST, and bank details.
2. Abianzo may verify documents and reject/suspend accounts for mismatch or risk.

## 4. Listing and Catalog Rules
1. Vendor must ensure product listing accuracy, lawful descriptions, and authentic pricing.
2. Vendor must maintain inventory updates and avoid false availability.
3. Prohibited products/services are not allowed.

## 5. Pricing, Tax, and Invoicing
1. Vendor sets lawful pricing and is responsible for GST/tax obligations.
2. Vendor authorizes Abianzo to collect payments and remit net payouts.

## 6. Orders and Fulfillment
1. Vendor shall process accepted orders within committed preparation timelines.
2. Quality, safety, packaging, and serviceability are vendor responsibility.

## 7. Refunds and Returns
1. Vendor must cooperate on refunds, replacements, and dispute handling.
2. Refund liability allocation is governed by Refund & Cancellation Policy and platform decisions.

## 8. Fees and Settlements
1. Abianzo may charge commissions, logistics fees, and related charges.
2. Payout timelines depend on payment realization, fraud checks, and dispute holds.

## 9. Vendor Conduct
1. No counterfeit, illegal, unsafe, or prohibited items.
2. No fake orders, fake reviews, manipulation, or off-platform diversion.
3. No harassment or abusive communication.

## 10. Platform Rights
1. Abianzo may delist products, hold payouts, or suspend accounts for policy violations, fraud, legal risk, repeated complaints, or quality failures.

## 11. Data Use and Confidentiality
1. Vendor must use customer data only for order fulfillment and support.
2. Vendor shall maintain confidentiality and prevent unauthorized sharing.

## 12. Intellectual Property
1. Vendor warrants rights to use all uploaded trademarks, images, and descriptions.
2. Vendor indemnifies Abianzo for third-party IP claims.

## 13. Indemnity and Liability
1. Vendor indemnifies Abianzo against claims from product defects, legal non-compliance, tax defaults, IP disputes, and customer harm.
2. Abianzo liability is limited to direct losses up to fees earned from disputed transactions.

## 14. Force Majeure
1. Performance delays due to uncontrollable events are excused during impact period.

## 15. Suspension and Termination
1. Abianzo may suspend/terminate vendor access for fraud, repeated SLA breaches, regulatory notices, or payment abuse.
2. Legal and financial obligations survive termination.

## 16. Governing Law and Arbitration
1. Governed by Indian law.
2. Arbitration seat: Bengaluru, Karnataka, under Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
