# Abianzo Vendor Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026  
**Privacy Contact:** privacy@abianzo.in

## 1. Scope
1. Applies to vendor app users and vendor representatives.

## 2. Data Collected
1. Identity and business data: name, phone, email, business name, GST, licenses.
2. Financial data: bank details, payout setup, settlement records.
3. Operational data: catalog, pricing, inventory, order processing logs.
4. Device and analytics data.

## 3. Purpose
1. Vendor onboarding, verification, payouts, tax compliance, fraud prevention.
2. Operational workflows and customer order fulfillment.

## 4. Third-Party Services
1. Firebase Authentication/FCM, Firestore/Realtime Database.
2. Payment providers and banking rails.
3. Maps and logistics support providers where required.

## 5. Sharing
1. Shared with customers/riders only for order execution.
2. Shared with regulators, tax bodies, or legal authorities when required.
3. No sale of personal data.

## 6. Retention and Rights
1. Data retained for compliance, tax, accounting, and dispute periods.
2. Vendors may request access/correction/deletion subject to legal retention constraints.

## 7. Compliance
1. IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023 aligned processing and safeguards.

## 8. Security
1. Role-based access, encrypted transport, operational logs, and abuse monitoring.

## 9. Governing Law and Dispute
1. Indian law; arbitration in Bengaluru.
