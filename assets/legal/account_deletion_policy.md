# Abianzo Account Deletion Policy

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026  
**Deletion Support:** support@abianzo.in  
**Privacy Contact:** privacy@abianzo.in

## 1. Deletion Request Process
1. Users can request deletion from in-app settings or by emailing support.
2. Identity verification may be required for security.

## 2. Processing Timeline
1. Deletion requests are typically processed within 7-30 days subject to verification.

## 3. Data Retained After Deletion
1. Financial transaction records, tax records, fraud logs, legal claims records, and compliance logs may be retained as required by law.

## 4. Effect of Deletion
1. User loses access to account history, saved preferences, and certain services.
2. Pending disputes or liabilities may require delayed closure.

## 5. Re-registration
1. Users may re-register later, subject to policy and risk checks.
