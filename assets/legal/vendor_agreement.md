# Abianzo Vendor Agreement

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026

## 1. Parties and Relationship
1. This agreement is between Abianzo and Vendor as independent parties.

## 2. Core Obligations
1. Vendor must maintain valid registrations, licenses, GST compliance, and lawful operations.
2. Vendor must ensure product authenticity, quality, pricing accuracy, and inventory integrity.

## 3. Commercial Terms
1. Abianzo may deduct commission, logistics, platform fees, and applicable charges before payout.
2. Settlement cycles, holds, and reversals apply for disputes/fraud/legal instructions.

## 4. Service Levels
1. Vendor shall accept and fulfill orders per agreed timelines and quality standards.

## 5. Prohibited Conduct
1. Counterfeit products, fake transactions, policy evasion, and data misuse are prohibited.

## 6. Data and Confidentiality
1. Vendor shall process customer data only for fulfillment and support.

## 7. Liability and Indemnity
1. Vendor indemnifies Abianzo against third-party claims from product defects, legal breaches, and tax non-compliance.

## 8. Suspension/Termination
1. Abianzo may suspend or terminate this agreement for policy violations, fraud, legal risk, or repeated service failures.

## 9. Dispute Resolution
1. Indian law governs.
2. Arbitration seat: Bengaluru, Karnataka.
