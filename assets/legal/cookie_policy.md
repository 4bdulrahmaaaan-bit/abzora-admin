# Abzora Cookie Policy

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026

## 1. Applicability
1. This policy applies to Abzora websites and web panels where cookies or similar technologies are used.

## 2. Cookie Types
1. Essential cookies for authentication and security.
2. Performance cookies for analytics and service improvement.
3. Preference cookies for language/session settings.

## 3. Purpose
1. Improve reliability, remember preferences, prevent fraud, and measure usage trends.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies
1. Analytics and infrastructure providers may set limited cookies as part of service delivery.

## 5. Control
1. Users can manage cookies through browser settings; disabling essential cookies may affect service functionality.

## 6. Compliance
1. Cookie use follows applicable Indian legal frameworks and DPDP-aligned transparency practices.
