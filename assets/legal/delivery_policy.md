# Abianzo Delivery Policy

**Effective Date:** 12 May 2026

## 1. Service Radius
1. Delivery is available only in supported zones and within configured service radius.

## 2. ETA and Timelines
1. ETAs are estimates based on traffic, weather, order load, and rider availability.

## 3. Delay Handling
1. Customers are notified for material delays.
2. Delays do not automatically guarantee refunds unless policy conditions apply.

## 4. Unreachable Customer
1. If customer is unreachable after reasonable attempts, order may be marked failed/cancelled.

## 5. Failed Delivery
1. Failed deliveries due to wrong address, non-availability, or non-response may attract limited/no refund based on case review.

## 6. Safety and Verification
1. Delivery may require OTP or other confirmation methods for secure handover.

## 7. External Factors
1. Weather, traffic, strikes, law-and-order issues, and force majeure may affect delivery performance.
